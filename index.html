Split Call uploader

A private script that uploads and schedules Shorts on the creator's own YouTube channel, Split Call (youtube.com/@SplitCall), replies to comments on those videos and reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics for a private report. It is used only by the channel owner and is not offered to other users.

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
