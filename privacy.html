Privacy policy – Split Call uploader

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Split Call uploader is a personal script run by the owner of the Split Call YouTube channel on their own computer. It has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set titles, descriptions and thumbnails, schedule publication, read the status of its own uploads and post comments on them, all on the owner's channel.

What it stores

The OAuth token and a log of uploaded video IDs are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No data from other YouTube users is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app's use of Google data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
